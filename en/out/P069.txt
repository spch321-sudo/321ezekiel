“Heart” (לֵב) appears 47 times in the whole book (37 verses, including לֵבָב). Idols can be taken into the “heart” (14:3), so the root of the sickness is in the heart; the stony heart must be exchanged for a heart of flesh (11:19; 36:26); people should “make” themselves a new heart (18:31), and God also “gives” a new heart (36:26): both sides are true. Finally, in 40:4, the prophet is told to “set your heart” on the temple: only one with a new heart can see the glory return.

“To live” (חָיָה) appears 42 times in the whole book (31 verses), spread over three places: the individual (16:6; 18:9; 18:32; 33:11), the community (37:3; 37:10; 37:14), and all of creation (47:9). God says “live” to the abandoned baby, says “why will you die?” to the wicked, says “you will live” to the dry bones, and says “the water will be healed” to the Dead Sea. The deepest promise of the whole book is this one word.
